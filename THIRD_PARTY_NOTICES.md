## Acquisition records and boundaries

[public/fonts/sources.json](public/fonts/sources.json) records original official
URLs, commits, download date, bytes and SHA-256 for every distributed binary.
[docs/font-audit.md](docs/font-audit.md) distinguishes distributed fonts from
visitor system fallbacks and the pre-existing, unused source-tree Inter asset.
The latter retains its original accompanying Inter-OFL.txt and is not copied to
the website output.

These notices do not certify fonts embedded in independent Logo artwork,
photographs, video subtitles or printed Lanyard textures. Those assets are
unchanged and have a separate review boundary documented in the audit.

The compact form composition and local pointer-accent idea are adapted from Manu Arora / Aceternity UI: https://ui.aceternity.com/components/signup-form and its signup-form-demo, input and label registries.
